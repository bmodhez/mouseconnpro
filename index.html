Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is MouseConnect / Remote Mouse?

It is a wireless utility that turns your mobile device into a fully functional wireless mouse, keyboard, and trackpad for your computer.

2. Do I need an internet connection to use it?

No! As long as both your phone and PC are connected to the same local Wi-Fi network or hotspot, no active internet connection is required.

3. How do I connect my phone to the PC?

First, install and run the Desktop Server on your PC. Then, open the mobile app and either scan the QR Code on your PC screen, or enter the PC's IP address manually.

4. Why is my phone unable to find the PC?

Please ensure that both devices are on the exact same Wi-Fi network. Also, check that your PC's firewall isn't blocking the application (Port 8765).

5. Is the app completely free to use?

Yes, the core features of the application are completely free to use. You can use the mouse, keyboard, and trackpad without any time limits.

6. Can I control multiple computers?

Yes! The app keeps a history of the last 7 connected computers. You can seamlessly switch between them using the Recent Devices list on your home screen.

7. Is my connection secure?

Absolutely. You can set a custom Connection Password in the desktop server preferences to ensure that only you can control your PC.

8. Does it work on Mac and Linux?

Currently, our primary desktop server is built for Windows 10/11, but Mac and Linux support is actively in development and coming soon.

9. Why is the mouse cursor lagging?

Lag is usually caused by a poor Wi-Fi signal. Try moving closer to your router, or connect your PC directly to the router using an Ethernet cable.

10. How do I use the Magic Trackpad gestures?

You can use two fingers to scroll vertically or horizontally, pinch to zoom, and tap with two fingers to simulate a right-click.

11. Why isn't the keyboard working in some PC games?

Some games block simulated inputs to prevent cheating. You can try enabling the "Compatible Keyboard Input" option in the Desktop Server preferences.

12. How do I change or remove my custom password?

Right-click the app icon in your PC's system tray (bottom right), select "Preferences", and click "Change" under the Connection Password section. Leave it blank to remove it.
