Privacy Policy for MouseConnect

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MouseConnect. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application and desktop software.

2. Information Collection and Use

MouseConnect operates locally over your Wi-Fi network. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, keystrokes, screen data, or usage metrics to external servers. All communication between the mobile app and the desktop software occurs strictly on your local network (LAN).

3. Network Permissions

To function properly, MouseConnect requires access to your local Wi-Fi network to discover and connect with your PC. The camera permission is solely required for scanning the QR code to facilitate this connection and no image data is saved or shared.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app may contain advertisements served by third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob, AppLovin, or Start.io). These networks may collect anonymous usage data and device identifiers to serve personalized ads in accordance with their own privacy policies.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our website.
